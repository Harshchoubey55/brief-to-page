Headline findings

  • 63% plan to add a second supplier for at least one critical part
  • 14% average rise in inbound freight cost over the last 12 months
  • 1 in 3 now hold more than 60 days of safety stock

Three things that changed this year

Second sources, not new countries

Most firms are not moving production. They are adding a backup supplier within the same region and paying a small premium for it.

Freight is the cost that surprised people

Raw material prices settled in 2026. Freight did not, and few budgets had room for it.

Stock is back on the balance sheet

Lean inventory is out of favour. Firms are holding more, and finance teams want to know what it costs.
